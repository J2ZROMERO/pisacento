# Rights, credits and asset provenance

`samples/manifest.json` contains original URLs, credits, declared licenses and SHA-256 hashes. Adjacent license text files are preserved. Read the manifest for each sample before redistribution. This export does not relicense third-party content.

VCSL/VSCO2 entries include CC0 recordings. Some tonejs-instruments recordings are declared CC BY 3.0 and require attribution. Historical synthetic voice files and illustrative substitutions are retained to preserve the snapshot and integrity checks; their presence does not make them active approved instruments. `instrument-samples.js` and the evidence allowlist determine the active Salsa set.

Bachata uses iASO Records' own Bandcamp streaming player. The album is all rights reserved. No Bachata tracks were downloaded into this project. Track IDs and credits are in `bachata-instruments.js`; the original publisher retains control of playback. Do not extract, redistribute or package these recordings without appropriate permission.

Links to teaching sources do not imply endorsement or permission to reproduce their videos, courses or books. Source summaries state what was reviewed. Heritage recognition does not certify this app.

Screenshots in `docs/screenshots` document development and may contain embedded-provider artwork or titles. Review them before using them publicly as marketing assets. No blanket license for the project source is assigned by this export; establish ownership and choose terms before publishing a derived project.
